Questions about pricing

How much does a website and back office cost?

DIY is $97 one time. Done For You is $497 one time. A Custom Build is $2,500, or $3,000 with a full Payhip store included. There are no monthly platform fees on any of them.

What is the difference between DIY, Done For You and Custom Build?

With DIY, you set up the template yourself using a step-by-step guide. With Done For You, we fill in the same template for you and set up your back office. With a Custom Build, we design the website and back office around your business, and it is not a template.

Can I start with DIY and upgrade later?

Yes. If you buy DIY and decide you want help, you can upgrade to Done For You within 45 days. Your $97 counts toward it, plus $10 for giving it a try, so you pay $390 more.

Do I own my website and my data?

Yes. Your site and your data live in your own accounts. Nobody can raise your price or lock you out.

Why is a Custom Build by application?

We take a limited number of Custom Builds each month, and each one is a working relationship that continues after launch. The application helps us make sure we can serve you well.

Do I have to pay monthly for a care plan?

No. Care plans are optional and prepaid for 6 or 12 months. They pay for our time and attention after launch. They are not a fee to keep your site online.

Are there other costs?

Hosting is free. If you want a custom domain name, you buy it yourself from a domain seller, and it usually costs about $10 to $20 a year. AE9 Labs does not sell domains. Airtable has a free plan, and a low-cost plan may apply later as your business grows.
