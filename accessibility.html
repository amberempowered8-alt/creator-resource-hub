Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Our commitment

AE9 Labs wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom, or other assistive tools. We're working to make this site meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which is the widely used standard for accessible websites.

What we've done

  • The site works on phones, tablets, and computers, and you can zoom in up to 200% or more without losing content.
  • Images have text descriptions (alt text) so screen readers can describe them.
  • Pages use clear headings and labels so they're easier to navigate with assistive technology.
  • Text and buttons are designed with readable color contrast.
  • You can move through the site and use its links and forms with a keyboard.

Known limitations

We're always improving, and some parts of this site may not be fully accessible yet. Some features, such as checkout, booking, or embedded forms, are provided by outside services. We choose them with care, but we don't control how they're built.

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, please let us know. We'll do our best to fix it, or get you the information or service you need another way.
