Yes! When you buy an AE9 Labs template, the code is yours. It's not rented from a platform, and nobody can raise your price or lock you out of your own site. Your site, your data, your business.

Yes. The setup guide walks you through every step, one at a time. And if you'd rather not touch it at all, AE9 Labs offers a done-for-you website and funnel setup where everything gets built and handed to you, and you still own all of it.

How is this different from a website builder?

Most popular website builders charge you every month for as long as your site is up, and your site lives on their platform. With AE9 Labs you pay once, you own the code, and you can host it free. Shop as you grow and build off what you know, instead of renting forever.
